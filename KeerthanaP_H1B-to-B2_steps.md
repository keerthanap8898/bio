## Critical Deadline Calculation

Known employment ceased / termination effective date: **2026-03-24**

Amazon’s immigration FAQ says the grace period for H-1B can be up to 60 consecutive days from termination or until the I-94 end date, whichever is shorter.

The I-797A attached I-94 date is **2028-10-10**, so the practical limiting date is the 60-day grace-period window.

| Counting method | Day 60 | Operational use |
|---|---:|---|
| Conservative count: 2026-03-24 as day 1 | 2026-05-22 | Use this as the filing deadline |
| Less conservative count: 2026-03-25 as day 1 | 2026-05-23 | Do not rely on this |

**Action rule:** File Form I-539 no later than **2026-05-22**. Prefer filing on **2026-05-20 or 2026-05-21**.
