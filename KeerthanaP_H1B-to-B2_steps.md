## Abstract

This document is my self-reviewable, step-by-step work plan for preserving lawful U.S. status after my H-1B employment ended. The practical fallback path is to file **Form I-539** to change status from **H-1B to B-2** before the safe grace-period deadline of **2026-05-22**, unless a new employer’s H-1B transfer is ready to file before then.

The purpose of this section is to let me re-read the plan from a fresh perspective, as if I were doing the process myself without relying on anyone else.

I-797A attached I-94: **2028-10-10**. | I confirm my employment ended on **2026-03-24**, my H-1B approval notice exists, my I-797A attached I-94 runs to **2028-10-10**, & my safe grace-period deadline is **2026-05-22**. | I know the filing deadline & status basis. |

### Abstract TLDR

1. I first verify my latest CBP I-94 at https://i94.cbp.dhs.gov/.
2. I treat **2026-05-22** as the safe filing deadline.
3. If no H-1B transfer is ready before that deadline, I file **Form I-539** for **H-1B to B-2**.
4. I request the shortest B-2 period I can honestly justify, usually **3 to 6 months**.
5. I check my proof-of-funds target before choosing the requested period:
   1. **3 months:** target **$20,000+**.
   2. **4 months:** target **$25,000+**.
   3. **6 months:** target **$35,000 to $40,000+**.
6. I upload identity, I-94, H-1B approval, I-20/cap-gap history, Amazon termination proof, proof of funds, address proof & a B-2 statement.
7. I personally review, certify, pay & submit.
8. I save every receipt.
9. I do not work unless a future lawful work-authorized status permits it.
10. I do not leave the U.S. while the change-of-status request is pending unless I am intentionally abandoning that route.
11. I keep the public GitHub README redacted & keep the real evidence packet private.

### Finance Evidence Decision Rule

1. If I request **3 months**, I should try to show **at least $20,000** in accessible funds.
2. If I request **4 months**, I should try to show **at least $25,000** in accessible funds.
3. If I request **6 months**, I should try to show **at least $35,000**, preferably closer to **$40,000**.
4. If my balance is below the target, I should either:
   1. Request a shorter B-2 period.
   2. Add a sponsor support letter.
   3. Add sponsor bank statements.
   4. Add proof of prepaid housing, if applicable.
   5. Add a realistic monthly budget showing reduced expenses.
5. I should avoid relying on severance, unemployment benefits, future job income, freelance income, consulting income, or unpaid invoices as proof of B-2 support.
6. I should explain any large recent deposits so they do not look unexplained or borrowed only for filing optics.

## Critical Deadline Calculation

Known employment ceased / termination effective date: **2026-03-24**

Amazon’s immigration FAQ says the grace period for H-1B can be up to 60 consecutive days from termination or until the I-94 end date, whichever is shorter.

The I-797A attached I-94 date is **2028-10-10**, so the practical limiting date is the 60-day grace-period window.

| **`S.No.`** | **`Counting method`** | **`Day 60`** | **`Operational use`** |

| 1 | **Conservative count: 2026-03-24 as day 1** | 2026-05-22 | Use this as the filing deadline |
| 2 | **Less conservative count: 2026-03-25 as day 1** | 2026-05-23 | Do not rely on this |

**Action rule:** File Form I-539 no later than **2026-05-22**. Prefer filing on **2026-05-20 or 2026-05-21**.

## I-539 Filing Workflow

### Online filing path

1. Open [USCIS I-539](https://www.uscis.gov/i-539).
2. Open [USCIS I-539 Online Eligibility](https://www.uscis.gov/i539online).
3. Confirm eligibility to file online as a single applicant.
4. Open [USCIS File Online](https://www.uscis.gov/file-online).
5. Log in to Keerthana’s USCIS account.
6. Start **Form I-539, Application to Extend/Change Nonimmigrant Status**.
7. Select **change of status**.
8. Current status: **H-1B**.
9. Requested status: **B-2**.
10. Use current CBP I-94 details from [CBP I-94](https://i94.cbp.dhs.gov/).
11. Upload evidence.
12. Review all dates, names, receipt numbers & addresses.
13. Verify fee on [USCIS G-1055 Fee Schedule](https://www.uscis.gov/g-1055).
14. Pay.
15. Submit.
16. Save final application PDF, confirmation page, payment receipt & receipt notice.

### Paper fallback path

1. Use paper only if online filing is unavailable or blocked.
2. Download current Form I-539 from [USCIS Form I-539](https://www.uscis.gov/i-539).
3. Download current I-539 instructions from the same page.
4. Verify fee at [USCIS G-1055 Fee Schedule](https://www.uscis.gov/g-1055).
5. Print single-sided unless instructions say otherwise.
6. Sign in ink.
7. Attach evidence.
8. Use USCIS’s current filing address from the official I-539 page.
9. Mail by trackable courier.
10. Save proof of delivery.
