I am now preparing a **six-month B-2 request through 2026-11-22**. The financial theory for the six-month request is not based on Chase cash alone. It is based on total documented personal assets of approximately **$39,393.01**, consisting of Chase checking/savings cash plus Pershing & Fidelity brokerage assets.

## I-539 Filing Workflow

### Online filing path

1. Open [USCIS I-539](https://www.uscis.gov/i-539).
2. Open [USCIS I-539 Online Eligibility](https://www.uscis.gov/i539online).
3. Confirm eligibility to file online as a single applicant.
4. Open [USCIS File Online](https://www.uscis.gov/file-online).
5. Log in to Keerthana’s USCIS account.
6. Start **Form I-539, Application to Extend/Change Nonimmigrant Status**.
7. Select **change of status**.
8. Current status: **H-1B**.
9. Requested status: **B-2**.
10. Requested end date: **2026-11-22**.
11. Use the I-797A H-1B approval notice as the primary H-1B change-of-status approval evidence.
12. Include CBP I-94 & travel history as supporting travel/admission evidence.
13. Upload identity/status evidence.
14. Upload Amazon termination evidence.
15. Upload Chase, Pershing & Fidelity financial evidence.
16. Upload the Financial Support Summary.
17. Upload the six-month B-2 statement.
18. Review all names, dates, receipt numbers, I-94 numbers & addresses.
19. Verify the current fee on [USCIS G-1055 Fee Schedule](https://www.uscis.gov/g-1055) or [USCIS Fee Calculator](https://www.uscis.gov/feecalculator).
20. Pay.
21. Submit.
22. Save final application PDF, confirmation page, payment receipt & receipt notice.

### Paper fallback path

1. Use paper only if online filing is unavailable or blocked.
2. Download current Form I-539 from [USCIS Form I-539](https://www.uscis.gov/i-539).
3. Download current I-539 instructions from the same page.
4. Verify fee at [USCIS G-1055 Fee Schedule](https://www.uscis.gov/g-1055) or [USCIS Fee Calculator](https://www.uscis.gov/feecalculator).
5. Print single-sided unless instructions say otherwise.
6. Sign in ink.
7. Attach evidence.
8. Use USCIS’s current filing address from the official I-539 page.
9. Mail by trackable courier.
10. Save proof of delivery.
